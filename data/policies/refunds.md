# Refund Policy

## Refund for delayed orders
If an order is delayed by more than 5 days past the expected delivery date, the customer is eligible for a full refund.

## Automatic refund limit
Refunds up to Rs 2000 for eligible delayed orders are approved automatically. Refunds above Rs 2000 need human approval.

## Refund timeline
Approved refunds reach the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.

## Non-eligible refunds
Orders that are not delayed, or delayed by 5 days or less, are not eligible for an automatic refund and are sent to a human agent.
