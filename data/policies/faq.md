# FAQ

## How do I track my order?
Share your order ID and the support agent will tell you the current status and expected delivery date.

## Can I change my delivery address?
The address can be changed only before the order is shipped.

## What payment methods are accepted?
UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

## How do I contact a human agent?
If the assistant cannot resolve your issue, your ticket is passed to a human agent who replies within 24 hours.
